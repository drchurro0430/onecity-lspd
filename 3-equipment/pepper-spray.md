# Pepper Spray

1. Pepper spray should only be used as a last resort when encountering violent or threatening individuals who pose a danger to officers or others.
2. Prior to deployment, Officers must assess the situation carefully, considering factors such as proximity, wind direction, and potential bystanders to minimize unintended exposure.
3. Officers should aim for the individual's face, specifically targeting the eyes, nose, and mouth, to maximize effectiveness and incapacitate the subject.
4. After deployment, officers must promptly provide medical attention and decontamination procedures to affected individuals, ensuring their safety and well-being.
5. To deploy pepper spray, simply obtain it from the armory, this will give you the desired pepper spray, as well as the antidote for the individual once in restraints. Antidote will be stored in the medical bags within your squad car.
