# Vehicle Equipment

Below you will see a list of equipment that is housed within Patrol Vehicles. These are found in ALL LSPD vehicles.

## Equipment

* ALPR System (Plate Reader)
* 2 Medical Bags (w/ AED)
* Agency Computer/Laptop
* High-Visibility Vest
* Portable Breathalyzer
* 2x Spare Body Armor
* Drug Recognition Kit
* 2x Fire extinguishers
* Spike Strips / Stop Sticks
* 8x Traffic Cones
* GSR Test Kit
* 4x Flares
* Service Rifle
* 12 Gauge Service Shotgun

Additionally, every LSPD Cruiser is equipped with armored inserts on each door, to include the trunk. These armored inserts are rated NIJ Lvl IIIA, meaning they are designed to stop high-velocity handgun caliber rounds up to .44 Magnum.&#x20;

Special Sub-Division Cruisers like those belonging to CIOD and ERT are instead equipped with NIJ Lvl III armored inserts capable of stopping up to 7,62x51mm NATO, also known as .308 Winchester.
