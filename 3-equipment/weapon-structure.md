# Weapon Structure

**All Probationary Officers and above should have the below in their patrol load out.**

| Lethal           | Less Lethal              | Equipment   |
| ---------------- | ------------------------ | ----------- |
| Service Pistol   | X26 Taser (3 Cartridges) | Flashlight  |
| 12 gauge Shotgun | Pepper Spray             | Switchblade |
| Service Rifle    | Baton                    |             |

All Service Rifles are to have the following attachments:

* Flashlight
* Grip
* Scope
* Suppressor \[Optional]
* Extended Magazine \[Optional]

The Shotgun & Pistol should have the following attachments:

* Flashlight

Below will be a list of the weapons you shall be allowed to use from designated menus:
