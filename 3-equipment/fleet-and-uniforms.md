An **Officer the Month** recipient is permitted to drive any main fleet vehicle during the following month. For example, a January Officer of the Month would be eligible to drive a 2022 Charger during the month of February.

\
A **Supervisor of the Month** recipient is permitted to wear plain clothing during the first week of the following month. For example, a January Supervisor of the Month would be eligible to wear plain clothing during the first week of February.
