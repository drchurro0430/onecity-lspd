# 2 - Department Structure

Please follow this from the base to the top to the best of your ability.

## Chain of Command - Heads Team

**Office of the Chief (Executive Staff)**

* Chief of Police
* Assistant Chief
* Deputy Chief

**Office of the Commander(Leadership Team)**

* Commander
* Colonel

**Office of the Major (High Command)**

* Major

## Chain of Command - Department Administration

**Department Administration**

* Captain
* Lieutenant

## Chain of Command - Supervisory Team

**Supervisory Team**

* Staff Sergeant
* Sergeant

**Supervisor In Training**

* Corporal

## Chain of Command - Patrol

**Patrol**

* Officer III
* Officer II
* Officer I
* Probationary Officer

**Trainee**

* Cadet
