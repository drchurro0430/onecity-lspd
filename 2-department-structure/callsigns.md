# Callsigns

Within LSPD, we use the alpha-phonetic K. This can stand for **KING** or **KILO**. You can decide what you call out over radio.

You will be issued with a number. This number will stay with you through your time within LSPD. The numbers that will not be given out to starting officers will be 15->01. They will be reserved for Department Leadership and Administration.

The setup is as followed:

**\[Rank Identifier] \[K] - \[Number]**

For example: **4K-87**.

You can call yourself 4K-87 or K-87 over radio - the choice is yours.

When being asked for a badge number, please present the Callsign in whole, EG: **4K-87**.

| Rank Identifier | Rank                               |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| 1               | Department Heads                   |
| 2               | Department Administration          |
| 3               | Department Supervisors             |
| 4               | Department Supervisors In Training |
| 5               | Department Patrol                  |
| 6               | Department Patrol In Training      |
