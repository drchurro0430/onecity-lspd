5/23/2023-

General overhaul to this document. 6/20/2023-

3.3 Weapons Policies overhaul. Provides more detail about pistols and rifles available to officers while patrolling and loosens certain restrictions.

Policies set in place forbidding the performance of law enforcement duties by Cadets as they are not commissioned law enforcement officers. They are civilian members of the department awaiting training.

6.4 Ride Alongs updated. This update gives the right to any Officers to deny a rider in their vehicle for any reason. This update also prohibits giving civilians ride alongs without approval from department administration.

7.2 Disciplinary Action has been implemented and will be updated as needed. - C. Coleman

7/1/2023-

3.3 Weapons Policies update. Implementation of the weapons from the /weapons menu have been integrated and guidance established. - C. Coleman

7/19/2023-

3.1 Vehicle Policies update. Expounded on authorized vehicles for patrol/pursuit use. Implementation of the term “Pursuit Rated” to explain what vehicles may and may not be used in vehicular pursuits to prepare for addition of the vehicle pursuit policy arriving soon. C. Coleman

7/22/2023-

3.1 Vehicle Policies has been updated to explain better what “earth tones” means as it pertains to unmarked vehicles.

5.3 Has been updated to reflect the requirement for 2 ride alongs for probationary Officers during their probationary period. C. Coleman

8/2/2023-

Under Section 3.2, Clarified exceptions to having badge numbers in steam names while on patrol in server

General update to Section 5 to clarify the process for Cadets and Probationary Officers.

Addition of Section 5.4 for Officers who transfer from other agencies and are assigned to probationary status. C. Coleman

Section 3.3 Updated to allow the STI DVC 2011 pistol for duty use.

Section 3 - Addition of pursuit policy and general cleanup of section three of this manual.
