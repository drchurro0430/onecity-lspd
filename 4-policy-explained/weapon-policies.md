# Weapon Policies

All officers will carry a baton, X26 taser, and service pistol on their duty belt. Officers shall only be allowed to attach the flashlight attachment on the Pistol or Combat pistol, no other modifications may be made to those firearms.

Officers may attach extended magazines, a scope, suppressor, grip and/or a flashlight to their service rifle. Weapon skins are permitted for Officer III and above. No modifications to the rifle may be made outside of this guidance. Officers will only be allowed to carry a maximum of two (2) spare taser cartridges and a third on the taser system itself.

* Use of weapons outside of the structure above should only be a last resort instance. Any negligence involved with using weapons or tools of or outside of this structure will result in a suspension of patrol duties and possibly a re-certification training. Any deviation from permitted weapon attachments found to be unnecessary will result in disciplinary action.

LSPD Officers are prohibited from using fully automatic weapons, unless trained properly by the SWAT subdivision within LSPD. Please use semiautomatic or single shot in place of this.

Staff may use any weapons they have access to. However, the Office of the Chief has the right to revoke this privilege.
