# Naming Policy

In-Game Name: Before going on duty, all Officers are required to have their steam name including both their badge number and trooper name (`5K-70 J. Smith`) from the One City Discord server.

Exception: Supervisors in training and above may have their rank abbreviated or spelled out and name (`Sgt F. Last` or `Sergeant F . Last`) in lieu of their badge number or may opt out of an identifier entirely for their steam name per the JDC policy. Supervisors in training and above should still have their Discord name match their steam name prior to patrol.

Approved abbreviations for ranks:

| Rank          | Rank                  | Rank           | Rank                  |
| ------------- | --------------------- | -------------- | --------------------- |
| N/A           | SSGT: Staff Sergeant  | MAJ: Major     | DCHF: Deputy Chief    |
| CPL: Corporal | LT: Lieutenant        | COL: Colonel   | ACHF: Assistant Chief |
| SGT: Sergeant | CPT: Captain          | CMD: Commander | CHF: Chief of Police  |
