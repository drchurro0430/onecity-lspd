# Tactical Equipment

Tactical Vests: Wearing vests is optional and may be done at a officer’s discretion. Any vest that has identifying marks such as “LSPD”, “Officer”, “State Police” “Police” may be worn. Officers may not wear vests that have agency identifying marks of SAHP, BCSO, FBI, etc.

Riot Helmet: Riot helmets are intended to provide safety to the most important part of the body in the event of dangerous situations; typically they are used alongside ballistic shields. Do not patrol with a riot helmet on; you may only deploy them in situations where you may justifiably use a tactical vest. Use hat/prop option 123/124 or 125/126 (Hold F11 - opens/closes face shield).
