# High-Crime Patrol Regulation

This regulation mandates that all patrol units operating within designated high-crime areas must deploy in pairs at all times. The objective is to enhance officer safety, improve situational awareness, and ensure immediate backup during potentially high-risk encounters. Paired patrols also support better decision-making and accountability in dynamic environments. Below is a visual description of current high-crime areas in SA.
