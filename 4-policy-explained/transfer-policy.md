# Transfer Policy

All incoming transfers are required to review all applicable SOPs upon entry into LSPSD, including but not limited to core department SOPs and any subdivision-specific SOPs they are assigned or later receive access to. It is the member’s responsibility to ensure familiarity with all policies prior to engaging in active duty.

In terms of expectations regarding policy review:

* Members are expected to complete this review within a reasonable timeframe following transfer.
* Members should limit active patrol involvement until they are confident in their understanding of applicable SOPs.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Failure to follow SOP due to lack of review will not be considered a valid excuse and may result in disciplinary action.
{% endhint %}
