# Uniform Policies

As stated above all officers part of the Los Santos Police Department are required to adhere to the designated 'Patrol Uniform Structure' at all times. This uniformity not only symbolizes our collective identity but also underscores our visibility and presence at any given scene.

Section 4 will provide further details regarding specific components of the uniform, along with any additional guidelines related to personal appearance standards;

Headgear: Officers must wear appropriate headgear that is stated in the Uniform Structure.

Sunglasses: All officers are permitted to wear sunglasses in uniform, so long as they are professional in appearance. Supervisors reserve the right to deny the officer’s selection of sunglasses/glasses due to a lack of professional appearance.

Coats: Officers may wear appropriate coats/ jackets in regards to weather conditions that are stated in the Uniform Structure.

Bodily Features: Officers are permitted so long as the tattoos are not offensive. Facial Hair is permitted and shall be kept neat and natural hair color. Hair shall be a natural color and kept neatly.

Unmarked/Plain Clothes: This is currently locked to Captain+, however, officers may use unmarked clothing if they are on a ride along with a Captain, so long as said captain approves the outfit. If officer wish to ride along unmarked with another department, it required the approval from your Division Commander (Commander+). - Does not include FTO Saturday
