# 4 - Policy Explained

Within this section of this document, you will be exposed to the policies we expect you to adhere to when active, patroling or representing LSPD. Failing to abide by these can result in a removal from LSPDD.

**Time Requirements -** Non-Supervisors are required to achieve at least 5 Hours of Patrol Time to be eligible for promotion. (0 Hours will lead to an Activity Check) Supervisors are required to achieve at least 10 Hours of Patrol Time to be eligible for promotion. (0 Hours will lead to an Activity Check)
