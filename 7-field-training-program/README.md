# 7 - Field Training Program

**Mission Statement & Standards**

* The mission of the FTO Department is to provide the highest quality training possible to Cadets seeking to become Officers, and to ensure high quality entry level law enforcement officers are produced.
* FTO’s are expected to remain professional in all matters pertaining to training events
* FTO’s are expected to utilize the provided training materials and make recommendations for “value added” training.

**Field Training Officer (FTO)**

Ensure Cadets are trained appropriately in accordance with existing approved training guides/materials, that the training is completed in a quality manner, and that they host sufficient training sessions. Perform in-game and classroom training alone, or with the assistance of other FTO. You must be experts in our training guidelines and department policies.
