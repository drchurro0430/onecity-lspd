# Training Instruction

A period of classroom instruction shall be given based on the Officer Basics presentation. The information provided by this guide is considered the minimum required knowledge required to be taught to the cadets. An FTO expanding on information provided by this guide is authorized so long as it does not lengthen the training time beyond what is considered a reasonable session. No FTO shall imply a rule regarding a procedure that is not defined in the guide or SOP.

Cadets in attendance will be asked to place themselves on push to talk to avoid distractions from their own microphones.

When providing classroom instruction, it is recommended that FTO presentations utilize a screen share link, or the screen share option within discord as it is the easiest way to ensure cadets and trainers are all on the same page. A “Present” tab is located in the top right corner of the document and will allow the presentation to be brought full screen and a laser pointer in lieu of a mouse option is available once this has been selected. The method for presentation is up to the individuals providing the classroom and using screen share IS REQUIRED. Other methods should be used when screenshare is ineffective or unavailable to cadets in training.

Cadets are allowed and encouraged to stop the instruction and ask questions. This creates a learning environment and may answer questions other cadets have but are too afraid to ask. We will ALWAYS foster a learning environment during cadet training.
