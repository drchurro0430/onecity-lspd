# Program Participation

* In order to join the program, any Officer II may contact an administrator within the LSPD Professional Development Bureau (PDB).
* Participating in the FTO Program is optional and is not required to be promoted to any position within the LSPD.

Personnel transferring to the LSPD who possess an FTO position in another department will not be permitted to maintain their FTO. They must undergo familiarization with our own program before proceeding to teach it.
