# 5 - Division Operations

LSPD offers a range of different divisions for Officer I+ to participate in.

Some of these divisions have rank requirements to get in; they will be highlighted in this section.

All Sub-Division affairs will be managed by The Commander's Office.

In LSPD, we operate four (4) different Bureaus. These will be inclusive of different sub divisions and will be lead by a Captain.

**SAHP Bureaus:**

* Special Operations Bureau
* Operations Bureau
* Technical Operations Bureau
* Professional Development Bureau
* Administrative Affairs Bureau (Managed By Heads)

LSPD subdivision policy: Members of LSPD are only allowed to be lead or co-lead of one subdivision at a time. This is to ensure full resources along with attention are given to their assigned subdivision.
