# LSPD Divisions

**Time Zone Based Divisions (NA & EU)**

To maximize efficiency and ensure consistent department operations across prominent regions of the playerbase, our LSPD Divisional structure is divided into **Pacific Standard (PST)** and **European (EU)** time zones. Each division is overseen by a Commander, responsible for managing their respective region’s affairs.

This system allows for:

* **Faster application responses** during peak regional hours
* **Active overwatch and supervision** within each time zone
* **Organised trainings and ride-alongs** scheduled for members’ availability
* **Balanced workload management** between leadership teams

Divisions shall not participate in independent promotional panels; they will still be a collective activity, however are expected to be more vocal on members who are within their Timezone.
