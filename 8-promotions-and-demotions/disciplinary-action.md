# Disciplinary Action

The Department Supervisory team reserves the right to clock off and suspend members of duty pending further investigation for policy violations.

The Department Administration team reserves the right to demote Officers for misconduct or actions not in line with the standards set forward in the LSPD SOP.

The Department Administration team reserves the right to remove members from the Los Santos Police Department if they believe the action warrants it, following the guidelines set forward in the Supervisor Manual.

The Chief reserves the right to override this decision and must provide a statement in writing to the Colonel's Office justifying this decision and follow on action to resolve the issue being had.\
\
**Officers** shall normally be issued strikes for misconduct when formally counseled by their chain of command. A 2nd strike received within a defined period of time shall result in demotion. A 3rd strike shall result in termination from the department. When evaluating strikes, the time between each occurrence shall be taken into account. Multiple strikes within a short period are considered a serious pattern of misconduct, while strikes accumulated over an extended period will be given reasonable consideration before action is taken.

**Officers** involved in an investigation, regardless of nature, shall not disclose any information regarding the investigation. Officers shall not directly message, contact, or attempt to influence the assigned investigator outside of official channels. Officers found in violation of these rules shall be issued a strike and marked ineligible for promotion until the next month. Any Officer who has received a strike during a period of consideration for promotion, or who is the accused party of an ongoing investigation for misconduct or violation of procedure, will not be promoted to the next pay grade until that matter is fully resolved.\
\
**Defining Period of Time**\
&#xNAN;**-** 2nd strike within 30 days of 1st = Demotion\
\- 3rd strike within 3 months of 1st = Termination

***

<mark style="color:red;">Lieutenant</mark> and above are the only supervisors who are authorized by The Office of the Chief to formally issue disciplinary action such as a Strike or a Suspension longer than 24 hours for misconduct.

<mark style="color:red;">Corporals</mark> and <mark style="color:red;">Sergeants</mark> shall only be allowed to inform a member of the LSPD that they are suspended for 24 hours and that the member is under investigation, to allow investigation of said circumstance involving misconduct or deviation from department policy. This suspension pending investigation is not disciplinary, and should never be treated as such but rather a means for protecting individuals being investigated from causing further issue for themselves or the agency. Initiation of a suspension pending investigation by a <mark style="color:red;">Corporal</mark> or <mark style="color:red;">Sergeant</mark> requires immediate notice to the Officer being investigated and notice delivered to a <mark style="color:red;">Lieutenant</mark> or higher immediately after the officer be suspended, the <mark style="color:red;">Lieutenant</mark> or higher that was notified is then responsible to either extend the suspension until the appropriate course of action is decided, or to return the suspended Officer to full duty status. Corporals and Sergeants need not suspend every officer they wish to investigate and this suspension should be weighed on a case by case basis, factors to take into consideration for suspension pending investigation are Officer conduct, Officer appearance, procedural issues and/or legality regarding circumstances of the Officer's actions.

<mark style="color:red;">Corporals</mark> and <mark style="color:red;">Sergeants</mark> will take the findings of their own investigation and provide it to a <mark style="color:red;">Lieutenant</mark> or above who will decide what course of punitive action if any should be taken in accordance with the LSPD table of penalties. <mark style="color:red;">Corporals</mark> and <mark style="color:red;">Sergeants</mark> are not permitted to deliver the punitive action/decision made by a <mark style="color:red;">Lieutenant</mark> or above, rather the individual who decides the punishment shall be the one to deliver the notice. <mark style="color:yellow;">There are no exceptions to this guideline.</mark>

<summary>Administrative Processes (Table of Penalties)</summary>

At a minimum one of five outcomes will result if misconduct has occurred:

<mark style="color:red;">Corporal+</mark>

**Informal counseling:** An informal counseling is only a conversation between the Corporal+ who is dealing with the report and the member being investigated. The party will discuss what occurred, why it was incorrect with references provided for the member to review and learn from, and a recommendation for corrective action. This method is nicknamed **Verbal Warning.**

**Letter of reprimand:** At a minimum, a letter of reprimand will include factual information regarding circumstances surrounding what the member did wrong, an explanation of why what the member did was wrong including references in SAHP documents, consequences if the issue persists & expectations moving forward. This method is nicknamed **Written Warning**.

<mark style="color:red;">Lieutenant+</mark>

**Strike:** At a minimum, a strike will include factual information regarding circumstances surrounding what the Officer did wrong, an explanation of why what the Officer did was wrong including references in SAHP documents, the consequences of receiving a strike & expectations moving forward.

**Extended Suspension:** Lieutenant+ may opt to suspend the Officer being investigated in accordance with the guidelines set forth in Supervisor Manual. Formal documentation in the form of a Strike must be made.

**Demotion:** Lieutenants+ may suggest a Officer being investigated be demoted, if that Officer  is to receive a Strike for their miscoduct.

**Removal:** Lieutenants+ may suggest a Officer being investigated be removed, if that Officer has done something especially egrigious.<br>
