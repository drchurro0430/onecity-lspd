# 8 - Promotions & Demotions

Every pay grade within the Los Santos Police Department has certain requirements for individuals to be qualified for a promotion.

Promotion boards shall convene between the 20th and 28th of every month.

Meritorious promotions will be considered between the 5th and 15th day of every month, and shall only be given for outstanding conduct and ability.\
\
Officers that display an existing pattern of misconduct, have received a strike in the last 60 days or are under investigation for a policy violation will not be promoted to the next pay grade until that matter is resolved.

TIG - Time in Grade\
TIS - Time in Service

<details>

<summary><strong>Promotions Within Patrol</strong></summary>

* Promotions from Cadet to Probationary Officer: will be achieved through completion of the Officer Basics Course within 60 days of hire. If the cadet fails to do so they will be removed from the department and required to wait their cooldown period before reapplying.<br>
* Promotions from Probationary Officer to Officer: will be achieved through completion of the mandatory 30 day Probationary Officer period and passing the Officer Interview with a member of the Department Administration.<br>
* Promotions from Officer I all the way to Officer III: will be based on activity. During this period it is expected that an officer continues to familiarize themselves with and broaden their understanding of the Los Santos Police Department's SOP, the San Andreas Penal Code and San Andreas Case Laws. Promotion in these ranks requires a minimum of two months TIG.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Promotions to and within Supervisory Team</strong></summary>

* Promotions from Officer III to Corporal: will require selection by a board of Supervisors, alongside a deeper understanding of the Los Santos Police Department's SOP, the San Andreas Penal Code and San Andreas Case Laws. It also requires Officer III to demonstrate the ability to lead others, have critical thinking skills and be able to quickly make informed decisions.
* Promotion from Corporal to Sergeant: will require selection by a board of Superviors and a display of comprehensive knowledge on the Supervisor Manual, the San Andreas Penal Code and San Andreas Case Laws as well as the Los Santos Police Department's SOP. On top of that Corporals must also complete 2 ride-alongs with a Sgt+ where they prove their skills as a Supervisor.
* Promotion from Sergeant to Staff Sergeant: will require selection by a board of Department Administrators and an advanced level of understanding on the Supervisor Manual, the San Andreas Penal Code and San Andreas Case Laws as well as the Los Santos Police Department's SOP. Additionally Sergeants will be required a minimum period of 2 months TIG.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Promotions to and within Department Administration</strong></summary>

* Promotion from Staff Sergeant to Lieutenant: will require selection by a board of Department Administrators ranked Captain or above and an expert level of understanding on the Supervisor Manual, the San Andreas Penal Code and San Andreas Case Laws as well as the Los Santos Police Department's SOP. Additionally, a Lieutenant shall be the CO (Commanding Officer) of a Subdivision, and acquire a Subdivision trainer certification prior to promotion. Staff Sergeants will also be required a minimum period of 2 months TIG.
* Promotion from Lieutenant to Captain: will require selection by High Command and above as well as a complete mastery of the Supervisor Manual, the San Andreas Penal Code and San Andreas Case Laws as well as the Los Santos Police Department's SOP. Additionally, a Captain shall be the CO (Commanding Officer) of a Bureau, ensuring its Subdivisions have their needs met and operate within the established regulations.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Promotion to High Command</strong></summary>

* Promotion from Captain to Major: will require selection by the Office of the Colonel and above with endorsement by the Office of the Commander, the complete mastery of the Supervisor Manual, the San Andreas Penal Code and San Andreas Case Laws as well as the Los Santos Police Department's SOP. It also requires Captains to exhibit visionary leadership, operational excellence, and policy level decision making while fostering inter-Bureau collaboration, maintaining high levels of professionalism, accountability, and community trust across all their Subdivisions. Additionally, a Commander shall be the CO (Commanding Officer) of a Division. Captains will also be required a minimum period of 2 months TIG.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Promotion to Leadership Team</strong></summary>

* Promotion from Major to Colonel and above: will require selection by the Office of the Chief.

</details>
