# Standards within the LSPD

* The Los Santos Police Deparment strives to maintain a positive working relationship with fellow law enforcement agencies by providing mutual aid in all jurisdictions.
* Los Santos Police Deparment Officers may assist any law enforcement officer in the course of their duties as seen fit by the officer themselves.
* All Los Santos Police Deparment Officers wearing a uniform or on duty, are to maintain a respectable level of professionalism during the conduct of any business that pertains to the LSPD. All Los Santos Police Deparment Officers are expected to do the right thing regardless of who is watching.
* All Los Santos Police Deparment Officers are expected to treat each other with fairness, dignity and respect. Those found disrespecting members of the department shall be considered for removal pending investigation.
* Officers who have demonstrated repeated poor interactions within the community's player base or who exhibit an inability to adhere with server rules and guidelines (Warns/Kick/Bans)will be reviewed for removal.
