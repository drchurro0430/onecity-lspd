# Respect & Courtesy

* Acknowledge that our primary mission is to serve the community, striving to create a safer society through intelligent enforcement of state laws and municipal codes.
* Cultivate a compassionate and community-oriented attitude, genuinely desiring to establish a safer environment for all.
* Recognize that community support hinges on the establishment of trust and confidence within our ranks, understanding the fragility of this support in the face of misconduct.
* We would also like to recognize that respect and courtesy ends where it ends. This goes both ways.
