# Accountability within the LSPD

* Uphold and apply the knowledge standards established in the Officer Basics Course, demonstrating integrity, accountability, and responsibility to the citizens entrusted to their service.
* Possess an in-depth understanding of the San Andreas Penal Code and the State's recognized Case Laws to facilitate their duties and public service.
* Embrace constructive feedback from peers and Senior Officers alike with an open and receptive mindset.
* Demonstrate the ability to identify issues and make informed decisions in response to them.
* Display a proactive commitment to continual improvement in knowledge, training, and education.
* Prioritize the safety of both the public and fellow Officers, with the welfare of the community as the utmost concern in the performance of their duties.
* Recognize their role as Law Enforcement officers and willingly identify themselves as such when requested by those they have detained.

<details>

<summary>LSPD Identification Policy</summary>

_LSPD Personnel are required to identify themselves through 1 unique identifier pertaining to the individual Officer. This can either be a Badge Number, Callsign or Last Name. The choice is yours._

_You only need to identify yourselves to people in your detention or people you have placed under arrest. You do not need to entertain random people that demand names and badge numbers._

_You must ID yourself at some point within the scene._

_If you identify yourself using your Last Name, you must also inform them of your rank within the Los Santos Police Department._
