# 1 - Enforcement & Ethics Codes

_**Department Leadership's Intent:**_

* Expand the Los Santos Police Department's number of Officers and equip them with the tools necessary to create a safer society.
* Train and maintain a team of disciplined and intelligent Law Enforcement professionals with a sincere desire to protect the public and maintain order in the State of San Andreas.
