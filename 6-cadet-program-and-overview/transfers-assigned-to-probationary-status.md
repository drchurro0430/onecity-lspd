# Transfers assigned to Probationary Status

Any transfer assigned to probationary status upon transfer from another law enforcement agency is still expected to learn the contents of Officer Basics Course linked in the Officer Basics Course, and the contents of the Probationary Officer follow on guide link at the end of the Officer Basics Course. If guidance was not prescribed in the transfer, probationary officers under this circumstance must still report to the commissioner after one month's time and pass an oral exam board with the Chief regarding Officer Basics/Probationary Officer Guide materials.

Anybody who transfers into LSPD; at any position; will be subject to an interview after 10 days within the LSPD. See: #transfer-questions.
