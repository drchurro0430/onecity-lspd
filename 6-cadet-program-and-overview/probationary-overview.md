# Probationary Overview

* A Probationary Officer is a <mark style="color:green;">**Certified member**</mark> of the LSPD and may perform law enforcement duties. They are expected to adhere to all LSPD guidelines and regulations, just like all other members of the agency.
* Receiving <mark style="color:red;">**ANY**</mark> punitive action can lead to the termination of the Probationary Officer.
* Probationary Officers may take LOA's up to 2 weeks IF they have completed 50% of tasks.

Probationary Officers are able to patrol on their own but they must perform ridealongs with a **F**ield **T**raining **O**fficer of the Los Santos Police to fulfill their Probationary Tasks.

<details>

<summary><mark style="color:blue;"><strong>Probationary Tasks</strong></mark></summary>

1. Properly and efficiently conduct Three traffic stops. This includes positioning of their vehicle and themselves, basic radio traffic, and overall protocol.
2. Respond to Three 911 calls in the correct manner with the correct code of response.
3. Effectively apprehend a suspect from any scene with a taser, baton, or any other less-lethal tool. And read a suspect their rights clearly with full understanding.
4. Properly and effectively conduct a vehicle pursuit. This includes initiation, proper radio traffic, and maintaining proper callouts, operating their motor vehicle in a professional and safe manner throughout the entirety of the pursuit.
5. Properly and effectively conduct a tactical pit manoeuvre to end a pursuit. This includes determining adequate conditions, proper positioning, followthrough, box (if available).
6. Participate in a foot pursuit. This includes keeping up with the suspect, providing person(s) description(s), direction, weapon status.
7. Properly file 3 arrest reports. This includes all charges, fine, jail time, and full narrative of the scene.
8. Properly Conduct a Code 5 Stop. This includes positioning the vehicles in the correct manner, communicating with other officers, and giving loud, clear, and easy to understand commands.
9. Properly provided medical aid treatment to a person. This includes utilising the medical script, administering the correct bandages to various injuries.
