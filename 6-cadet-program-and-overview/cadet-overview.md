# Cadet Overview

* A Cadet Officer is a N**on-Certified** member of the LSPD and may not perform law enforcement duties or functions.
* A Cadet can be terminated <mark style="color:$danger;">**WITHOUT NOTIFICATION**</mark> for ANY violation of policy.
* Cadets <mark style="color:red;">**MAY NOT**</mark> patrol; they must ride along with a commissioned officer of the Police Departmnent if they wish to see duty time. Cadets may be allowed to clock in to monitor radio traffic, however they are not permitted to be armed or wear clothing that resembles that of law enforcement while riding along. Cadets may not handcuff, search, detain or interview suspects or witnesses. Cadets are forbidden from performing any law enforcement functions.
* Members at the position of Cadet may not take LOA's. The Cadet period will remain regardless of activity. Failing to get trained within the time frame will result in removal.

Cadets are **REQUIRED** to have their callsign in their steam name during training events, or when riding along. You may be kicked from the server during training events or ride along. If you do not have your callsign in your steam name.

Cadets should see the UNIFORM STRUCTURE for the items of clothing they are permitted to wear.
