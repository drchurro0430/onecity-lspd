# Officer Basics & Probationary Officer Course

Officer Basics Course (OBC) is conducted often and at an FTO’s convenience. OBC training events are announced and planned in #training-schedule in the LSPD category of the Discord. Training shall consist of a classroom based on the Officer Basics Course in the LSPD Cadets pinned channel. In-game training shall be the practical application of all things covered within the basic training guide. Officer Basics Course is very thorough and will cover a lot of material. Cadets are responsible to ensure they can complete the duration of the training and plan accordingly to complete the criteria required. Every effort shall be made to train Cadets by the FTO department, if you are unable to find a training you can attend, reach out to an FTA and they will assist you with accomplishing your cadet training

After completing the Officer Basics Course, the individual, now referred to as a probationary officer, will be assigned homework as part of the Probationary Officer period that can be found back in the Probationary Officer Course.
