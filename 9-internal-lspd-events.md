# 9 - Internal LSPD Events

**Throwback Thursday**

During this time period, ALL members of the LSPD (excluding Sub Division Operations \[apart from TEU and K9, they should still run utility Class C]) are requested to use the Utility Class C uniform. Vests are optional and can be worn at the Officers discretion. Cowboy hats and boots are encouraged. You do not need a tazer equipped if you do not want to.

You may use any variant of the Revolver and the Double Barrel Shotgun.

We would like to see the retro fleet being used or the retro livery's that are on the main LSPD fleet. There should be no unmarked patrolling during this time.

The time's are as followed:

* **6:00 PM GMT → 2:00 AM GMT+8 (next day)**
* **3:00 AM GMT → 11:00 AM GMT+8**
* **1:00 PM EST → 2:00 AM GMT+8 (next day)**
* **12:00 PM CST → 2:00 AM GMT+8 (next day)**
* **7:00 PM CET → 2:00 AM GMT+8 (next day)**

**Formal Friday**

During this time period, ALL members of the LSPD (excluding Sub Division Operations \[apart from TEU and K9, they should still run class A]) are requested to use the Class A uniform. Vests are optional and can be worn at the Officers discretion.

We would like to see the regular fleet being used as much as possible with the respective LSPD livery. There should be no unmarked patrolling during this time.

The time's are as followed:

* **6:00 PM GMT → 2:00 AM GMT+8 (next day)**
* **3:00 AM GMT → 11:00 AM GMT+8**
* **1:00 PM EST → 2:00 AM GMT+8 (next day)**
* **12:00 PM CST → 2:00 AM GMT+8 (next day)**
* **7:00 PM CET → 2:00 AM GMT+8 (next day)**
